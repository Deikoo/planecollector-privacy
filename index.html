Privacy Policy

This page explains what PlaneCollector stores on your device, what is saved to your account, and what other players can see. It covers the Android and iOS apps.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Accounts: Google, Apple, and Guest Mode

You can sign in with Google or Apple, or, when the app offers it, continue as a guest. A guest account is an anonymous account with its own id. It is not linked to Google or Apple until you choose to link one. Linking keeps that same account, including captures and purchases already on it. We do not merge it with a different PlaneCollector account that already uses that Google or Apple identity.

If the sign-in provider shares them, we store the display name, profile-picture URL, and email address with the sign-in account. The email address is not shown on the leaderboard. If no name is available, the profile name defaults to “Pilot”. Country is taken from the Google locale when present, otherwise from the device locale, and is used for the country leaderboard. You can change the display name and country in the app.

Guest accounts. A guest account lives in that session on the device. If you sign out, uninstall the app, clear the app’s data, or lose the device before linking Google or Apple, you may not be able to open that account again. Cloud data for a guest account you can no longer sign into is not automatically deleted. Email us if you need help identifying it.

2. On your device

The app keeps a local database of your collection: capture records, photos, settings, and game progress. If you turn on the optional Planespotters.net photos, the email you enter is stored on the device. If you connect an optional OpenSky account, the client id and client secret are stored on the device and sent only to OpenSky to request aircraft data.

Uninstalling the app removes this local data. Signing out or deleting the account also clears the local collection for that account.

3. In your account

When you are signed in, including as a guest, the app syncs the collection to our database (Supabase) so the game, score, and purchases can follow that account. A capture record can include:

  • time of the capture, score, and rarity;
  • registration, aircraft type, operator, callsign, flight number, route, and airports;
  • the device’s latitude and longitude at the time of the capture;
  • the aircraft’s reported latitude, longitude, altitude, and speed, when we have them;
  • the storage path of a backed-up photo, if you uploaded one.

Your profile stores the display name, avatar URL, country, total score, capture count, weekly results, and subscription tier (basic or premium). Purchase tokens or transaction ids are stored only to check that a store purchase is genuine and to turn on Premium or a consumable. We do not receive your card number.

Other signed-in players cannot read your capture records. They can read profile rows, which is how the leaderboard works. See section 6.

4. Photos

Aircraft recognition runs on your device with Google ML Kit. The image used for recognition is not sent to us for that step.

The photo itself is stored on the device. Premium accounts can also back it up. Cloud photo backup is on by default for Premium and can be turned off in Settings. When it is on, the spotting photo is compressed to a JPEG of about 50 KB or less and uploaded to a private storage area under your account id. Only that account can read it. A photo uploaded while you had Premium can still be restored later if the subscription ends. Extra photos you add to a card from your gallery stay on the device and are not part of this backup.

5. Location

To find aircraft near you, the app sends a bounding box — a rectangular area, not a saved trail of your movement — to the OpenSky Network. That lookup is not stored as a location history.

When a capture is saved to a signed-in account, the device coordinates at that moment, and the aircraft coordinates when known, are stored with that capture. They are part of your collection, not part of the leaderboard.

6. Leaderboard and profiles

Signed-in players can see other players’ display name, country, score, and capture counts on the all-time, weekly, and country leaderboards. The weekly board can also show whether a place is in line for Reward Points. Capture details, coordinates, and photos are not shown there.

Profile rows are readable by any signed-in player, not only the fields drawn on the leaderboard. Those rows also include the account id, avatar URL, and subscription tier. They do not include your email address.

8. Ads, product events, and crash reports

PlaneCollector uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, in exchange for an extra capture. AdMob may collect an advertising id and device information for ad personalisation, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. On iOS the app asks for App Tracking Transparency before AdMob starts. You can refuse that, and you can turn off personalised ads in the device settings.

We do not use a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK such as Firebase or Sentry. The app does send a small amount of first-party diagnostics to our own database when you are signed in:

  • product events, such as finishing onboarding, opening the camera, attempting a capture, or tapping invite, together with your account id, app version, build number, and platform;
  • a daily activity registration with app version, build number, and platform;
  • crash and operational error reports with app version, platform, OS version, device name, screen name, error text, and stack trace. Email addresses and tokens are removed before upload. Your account id is included when you are signed in.

9. In-app purchases and payments

PlaneCollector offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions and consumable items) processed entirely by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We never receive, store, or process your payment card details — all billing is handled by Apple or Google under their respective payment policies.

Upon a confirmed purchase, our server receives a purchase token or transaction ID from the platform solely to verify the purchase is genuine and to activate your subscription or consumable. This token is stored in our database (Supabase) linked to your account and is used only for subscription status checks. It is deleted when your account is deleted.

10. Data security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data:

  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Cloud data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS), which provides encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Capture records and backed-up photos are limited to your own account.
  • Profile rows can be read by other signed-in players, as described in section 6.
  • Server-side functions run with access limited to the signed-in account.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe there has been a security incident, please contact us immediately at deikoo@msn.com.

11. How long we keep data, and deletion

Local data stays on the device until you uninstall the app, sign out, or delete the account. Signing out clears the local collection.

While you are signed in, including as a guest, Profile has Delete account. That permanently deletes the sign-in account, the profile, the capture records, and the backed-up photos for that account. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the stored Apple refresh token when Apple’s service is available. A temporary Apple outage can delay deletion until you try again.

Diagnostic reports are not deleted as their own step. Some are kept with the account id removed when the account is deleted, and some may remain until we remove them on request. Email us if you want those deleted as well. A guest who can no longer sign in cannot press Delete account, and should email us instead.

12. Children

PlaneCollector is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will delete it.
