Privacy Policy

We built PlaneCollector with privacy as a first principle. This page explains exactly what data is collected, why, and what never leaves your device.

Last updated: July 2026

1. What we collect

PlaneCollector stores your captures, scores, and collection data locally on your device (SQLite). If you sign in with Google, your score and capture counts are synced to our cloud database (Supabase) solely to power the global leaderboard. No photos ever leave your device.

2. Camera

Photos are taken to identify aircraft. AI inference runs entirely on-device using Google ML Kit — no image data is ever transmitted. Photos are stored only in the app's local directory and are never uploaded to any server.

3. Location (GPS)

Your location is used to find aircraft near you via the OpenSky Network. Only a bounding box (a rectangular area, not your exact coordinates) is sent in the request. Your location is never stored on any server and is never tied to your account.

4. Third-party services

Service Data sent Purpose
OpenSky Network Lat/lon bounding box Find nearby aircraft
hexdb.io ICAO24 hex code Look up registration & type
FlightRadar24 Aircraft registration Fetch route data
Planespotters.net Registration + email (User-Agent) Aircraft photos — opt-in only
Supabase Score & capture count Leaderboard (when signed in)
Wikimedia Commons Aircraft type name string Fetch generic type image
Planespotters.net note: This feature is disabled by default. If you enable it in Settings, you provide an email address that is sent as a User-Agent header to Planespotters.net only — per their API terms. It is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any other service.

5. Analytics & tracking

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or telemetry SDK. No user behaviour is tracked in any way.

What is never sent: user photos, device ID, precise GPS point, email to any service other than Planespotters.net (opt-in).

6. Data retention

All local data (captures, photos, settings) is stored on your device and removed when you uninstall the app. Cloud data (score, capture count) is deleted on request — see the contact section below.

7. Children

PlaneCollector is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact & data deletion

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your cloud data? Open an issue in the repository or send an email and we will respond within 30 days.
